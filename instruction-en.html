How to connect to Guard VPN

1
Download the app
Choose the right app for your device:
📱 Happ (Android) 🍏 Happ (iOS) 📱 v2rayNG (Android) 💻 Hiddify (Win/Mac/Linux)
2
Get the link
Go back to the main screen and click «Get config». Copy the link you receive.
💡 Example link: https://your-server/sub/abc123xyz
3
Add subscription
For Happ:
Open app → tap «Subscription» → «Add Subscription» → paste the link → «Add»
For v2rayNG:
Open app → tap «+» → «Import Subscription» → paste the link → «OK»
4
Connect
Select a server from the list and tap the connect button. When connected, the status will change to «Protected».
⚠️ Not working?
• Try switching to another server
• Refresh the link — click «Get config» again
• Reinstall the app
• Contact support
